What CBAM will cost you, and what you still do not know

Enter the goods you import into the EU. This tool tells you whether you cross the 50 tonne de minimis threshold, prints the certificate cost arithmetic one step at a time from figures you supply, and refuses to invent the emissions figures you do not have. Where a number is missing it names the number, names who holds it, and gives you a list you can send to the supplier.

    What this tool does not know

      This is an estimate for planning. It is not legal, tax or customs advice. The numbers it prints are only as good as the figures you enter and the constants listed in section 08. Confirm every figure against the current text of Regulation (EU) 2023/956 as amended, the implementing acts in force, and your own advisers before you file anything or commit money.
