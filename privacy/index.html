What this site does not do

No analytics. No advertising. No tracking pixel, no fingerprinting, no social media button that phones home. No cookie is set by this site at all, which is why there is no banner asking you about one. The typefaces are served from this site rather than from a font network, so opening a page here sends your address to Google exactly never.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Like every web server, theirs records the requests it answers: your IP address, the time, the page asked for, and what your browser says it is. I never see those records and they are not joined to anything else. The interest that justifies it is having a site at all and keeping it standing up.

GitHub is in the United States. It is certified under the EU US Data Privacy Framework and additionally uses the standard contractual clauses.

Art. 6 (1)(f) · Art. 45 · Art. 46 (2)(c) GDPR

Getting in touch

The contact page is two mail links and nothing else. Pressing one opens your own mail programme with my address already in it, and nothing is sent until you send it. The previous edition of this site, which is still served under /run, does carry a form: what you type into it never leaves your browser either, because the button hands it to your own mail programme, and the two links beside it put the same text into a Gmail or Outlook compose window in your own account. Nothing is transmitted until you press send in a window of your own. So the only thing that reaches me is the email you decide to write, and it reaches me the way any email does. It stays in my mailbox as long as the conversation is live and for as long afterwards as the law asks me to keep it.

Art. 6 (1)(b), (f) GDPR

What your browser remembers

Four settings, and none of them leaves your machine: which of the three languages you chose, the light or dark theme, the text size, and whether reading mode is on. The pages under /run, which are the previous edition of this site, also remember the season you set the game to, whether you muted it, and whether you have been there before. All of it is local storage in your own browser, none of it is a cookie, and none of it is sent anywhere. Clear your site data and it is gone.

Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, ask me to restrict what I do with it, object to it, and ask for a copy in a portable form. In practice I hold nothing but the emails you have sent me. You can also complain to a supervisory authority; for Munich that is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht. Nothing here decides anything about you automatically and there is no profiling.

Art. 15 to 21 · Art. 77 GDPR
