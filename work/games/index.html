Game theory

Six small games that are played rather than read.

The Long Game20 to 30 min

Why a future changes the arithmetic of the present.At the end of every season a neighbour comes to the fence, and you decide whether your sack goes over it or stays on your step.

Open
The Stag Hunt5 to 10 min

The trap is doubt rather than greed. Two equilibria, and only one of them is good.A stag is worth five days of eating and cannot be taken by one person. A hare is worth two days and can.

Open
The Ultimatum5 to 10 min

Fairness as a force in real bargaining rather than a preference.You say how ten coins are divided and the stranger says yes or no. If no, neither of you takes anything home.

Open
The Commons8 to 12 min

The tragedy as a result about rules rather than about people.The pasture belongs to all four households and to none of them, and nobody asks permission because there is nobody to ask.

Open
The Beauty Contest5 to 8 min

How deep you should reason about other people.Everyone chalks a number from zero to a hundred. The winning number is two thirds of the average.

Open
Matching Pennies5 to 10 min

Sometimes the only unexploitable strategy is to be unpredictable.If the two coins agree she takes yours, and if they differ you take hers.
