Tools for a single job

Seventeen jobs that otherwise need paid software or a spreadsheet built by hand. Each one is a tool that opens in a browser and does that job, and 870 checks written by hand hold them up.

17 of 17
Dotsheet58 checks

Text into Unified English Braille, and the braille back into text, so you can read what it actually says.

Open
Sheetwise72 checks

Puts the pages of a PDF onto press sheets in the order a folded and stitched book needs.

Open
Cutplan43 checks

Turns a drawing into instructions for a laser cutter, cutting the holes before the outline so the part does not drop out first.

Open
Loudmeter36 checks

Measures how loud an audio file is against the broadcast standard, and says how much to turn it up or down.

Open
Cutfill45 checks

How much earth has to come out and how much has to go in to bring a site to the level you want.

Open
Cuecheck69 checks

Checks a subtitle file against the reading speed and timing rules a broadcaster applies.

Open
Truescale57 checks

Four body measurements in, a skirt pattern out, tiled across A4 sheets you can print and tape together.

Open
Setter50 checks

Fills a crossword grid from your own word list and keeps the grid symmetrical.

Open
Boardplan37 checks

Lays parts out on sheet material in cuts a panel saw can actually make.

Open
Buildup40 checks

The layers of a wall in, how much heat it loses out, to the standard building control asks for.

Open

Open
