Where the code lives

All of it is public, all of it Python under the MIT licence unless a row says otherwise. The four at the top hold the instruments; the rest are the research, the routing layer, the skill packs and this site.

  • esg-toolkitCarbon decomposition, target pathways, abatement cost and CBAM embedded emissions. Four of the five instruments come from here.
  • analyst-toolkitMarket sizing from two directions, and a discounted cash flow with the terminal value taken apart.
  • unitguardUnit and dimension safety for energy and emissions arithmetic. It refuses rather than guesses.
  • open-climate-dataOne interface over public climate, energy and emissions datasets, where every value carries its source, its version and its date.
  • simearesearch, CC BY 4.0 for the writingThe framework note and the positioning note, with the models that test them. Neither has been peer reviewed and the repository says so.
  • rotaThe routing layer over the skill packs, and the only repository here with a runtime.
  • agent-kit and eleven moreTwelve repositories of working method packaged as skills, one per trade: finance, sustainability, research, writing, risk, strategy, presentations, spreadsheets, careers, sites, repositories, and the conventions the other eleven follow.
  • keremozdemirra.github.iothis site, as it is servedThis site, as it is served. Nothing is compiled out of reach: what a browser receives is what the repository holds.
