ESG and finance instruments

The kind of arithmetic that otherwise takes a spreadsheet, a consultant, or both: CBAM, embedded emissions, reduction pathways, provenance and valuation.

  • CBAMRunningWhether an importer has to report under CBAM at all, and what the certificates cost if they do.
  • Embedded emissionsRunningHow much carbon is inside a product by the time it is finished, traced back through everything that went into it.
  • PathwayRunningA reduction target, and the total carbon it still lets you emit on the way there, which is the part a target leaves out.
  • ProvenanceRunningAn emission factor that carries where it came from, and keeps carrying it through every calculation it enters.
  • AnalystRunningA valuation that shows how much of its answer rests on a guess about the far future, and a market sized from two directions at once.
