02

ESG and finance instruments

Five instruments for the kind of arithmetic that otherwise takes a spreadsheet, a consultant, or both: a CBAM screen, an embedded emissions trace, a reduction pathway, a provenance record and a valuation. Pick one and it runs in the panel.

  • CBAMRunningWhether an importer crosses the 50 tonne de minimis threshold, and what the certificates cost.
  • Embedded emissionsRunningEmissions traced back through a precursor chain, with the attribution reconciling exactly to the figure it explains.
  • PathwayRunningA reduction target and the cumulative carbon budget it implies, not just its endpoint.
  • ProvenanceRunningA cited emission factor that carries its source, and refuses a timestamp with no timezone on it.
  • AnalystRunningA discounted cash flow that prints how much of its answer the perpetuity carries, and a market sized top down and bottom up side by side.
