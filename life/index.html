My life as a game

A hand drawn game of one year in the life of a single tree. A museum sits under its roots, Istanbul on one side and Munich on the other. No images and no video: everything is drawn line by line as you move through it.

What it is

Scrolling down runs a year: summer, the turn, the fall, dusk, and then it goes underground to the roots and the doors. Nothing in it runs on a timeline. Every leaf's flight is calculated from your scroll position, so scrolling back up runs it exactly in reverse and the leaves return to their branches.

Nothing in it depends on the clock except a light idle sway, which is the reason it can be run backwards at all.

The world

The cover is not the whole map. Scroll up and the sky opens: clouds, a hot air balloon towing a banner, birds circling a thermal, and at night the stars. Drag sideways and the camera pans off the meadow. West lies Istanbul, with the Bosphorus, Kız Kulesi, a working ferry and the Beyazıt gate. East lies Munich, with the Alps, the Frauenkirche, the Eisbach and its surfer, a beer garden, the U-Bahn and TUM.

Underground there is more than roots: a cellar, a well whose rope reaches the water table, an ant colony with branching galleries, a mole, a sleeping rabbit, a buried amphora, an aquifer with a blind fish in it, and a museum of thirteen objects in three bands, the deepest being the earliest.

Six ways in

Six things in the world are doors as well as scenery. The wooden sign on the pier in Istanbul, the U-Bahn stairs in Munich, the banner behind the balloon, the door of the house, a workbench with a saw leaning on it, and a game table with the stones of a game nobody finished still on the board.

How it is built

One HTML file for the whole game. No framework, no images, no video, no tracking. The tree, the roots and the grass are grown by a seeded random walk, so the same tree comes back every time.

The game is built in its own repository and nothing publishes from it until a twelve gate audit passes. A release is blocked that carries a credential pattern or a personal data value, that leaves a string untranslated, or that ships a dictionary which is not symmetric across the three languages. One gate rebuilds every page, one reads every built page back as JavaScript, and one refuses a CV older than the page it was printed from.

Where it lives

It plays here, and it also runs on its own, in English, Turkish and German. The notebook at the bottom of it explains every hidden thing in it. Nothing in there is a riddle: if something is listed, it happens.
