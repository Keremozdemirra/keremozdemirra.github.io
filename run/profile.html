I study Sustainable Management & Technology at the Technical University of Munich, and Economics at Istanbul University alongside it. The pairing is deliberate: one degree is about how industry has to change, the other about how the money behind it behaves.

The work I care about is the kind that can be checked. An emission inventory someone else can audit. A market model where every assumption has a name on it. A tool that refuses to guess when a number is missing, and says which number and who holds it.

I am building CoLaunch with a small team, and I keep a set of open-source calculators for carbon accounting and financial analysis. Five of them run live on the projects page. Everything published under my name carries its source.
