Profile

Munich. Two degrees in parallel, six stints of real work, one company being built.

I study Sustainable Management & Technology at the Technical University of Munich, and Economics at Istanbul University alongside it. The pairing is deliberate: one degree is about how industry has to change, the other about how the money behind it behaves.

What I actually enjoy is making things hold up. An emission inventory that someone else can check. A market model where every assumption has a name on it. A tool that refuses to guess when a number is missing, and says exactly what is missing instead. Most of my repositories exist because a spreadsheet somewhere failed silently and I did not want that to happen twice.

I am building CoLaunch with a small team, and I keep a set of public instruments for carbon accounting and financial analysis. Five of them run live on the projects page. Everything published under my name carries its source.
